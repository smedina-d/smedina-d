## 🆕 Latest: an AI content pipeline with a human in the loop

**Real industry news in, professional LinkedIn posts out — and nothing goes live until I tap _Approve_.**

An **n8n** workflow that pulls news from RSS feeds, drafts a post with **Claude** in a calm, professional tone, finds a matching image, and sends the draft to me on **Telegram**. Only if I approve it does it publish to LinkedIn. What used to take hours now takes minutes.

![n8n](https://img.shields.io/badge/n8n-workflow-EA4B71?style=flat&logo=n8n&logoColor=white)
![Claude](https://img.shields.io/badge/Claude-API-D97757?style=flat&logo=anthropic&logoColor=white)
![Telegram](https://img.shields.io/badge/Telegram-approval-26A5E4?style=flat&logo=telegram&logoColor=white)
![LinkedIn](https://img.shields.io/badge/LinkedIn-publish-0A66C2?style=flat&logo=linkedin&logoColor=white)

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[📰 RSS news] --> B[🧠 Claude drafts the post]
    B --> C[🖼️ Matching image]
    C --> D[📲 Telegram: Approve?]
    D -->|Approved| E[💼 Publishes to LinkedIn]
    D -->|Rejected| F[✋ Discarded]
```
